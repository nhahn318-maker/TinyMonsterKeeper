# Gameplay Map Final Design Blueprint V2

Reference image: `gameplay-map-final-design-blueprint-v2.png`

## Visual hierarchy

1. Central cooking plaza: primary gameplay hub, with the pot framed by two trees, a stone edge, a notice board and a bench.
2. Northern mine: strongest distant landmark, replacing the two competing generic rock areas.
3. Western bamboo sanctuary: one bamboo pavilion, one lantern and three asymmetric bamboo masses.
4. Eastern farm: compact enclosure with three crop beds and a deliberately open work yard.

## Path structure

- Keep one wide route from the south gate through the plaza toward the mine.
- Use narrower curved branches from the plaza to the bamboo sanctuary and farm.
- Maintain at least one large clear roaming area inside every district.
- Keep interactive objects away from path intersections so touch targets do not overlap.

## Decoration rules

- Preserve roughly 40 percent open grass as visual breathing room.
- Place small props in clusters of three, then leave a quiet gap.
- Build the forest edge from several irregular canopy masses instead of one repeated bush wall.
- Use flowers only as accents near entrances and landmarks.
- Avoid evenly spacing grass, mushrooms, rocks, bamboo or identical trees.

## Suggested new art

- Mine entrance with timber support.
- Short mine rail and mine cart.
- Small excavation crane or ore workbench.
- Bamboo pavilion or moon gate.
- Wooden notice board.
- Simple log bench.
- Two or three alternate forest canopy silhouettes for edge variation.

## Build order

1. Paint only terrain and the three path widths.
2. Place the plaza, mine, bamboo pavilion and farm enclosure.
3. Verify camera routes, monster roaming space and click access.
4. Add large tree and bamboo masses.
5. Add resource nodes and gameplay objects.
6. Add sparse decorative clusters last.
